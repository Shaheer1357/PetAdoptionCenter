📖 Essential Pet Care Handbook

Click on any pet category to expand guidelines and helpful advice.

Nutrition: Feed high-quality protein meals twice daily according to age and body weight.

Exercise: Ensure at least 45 minutes of active walk or play daily.

Veterinary: Schedule annual vaccinations, flea/tick preventatives, and dental checks.

Litter Box: Keep clean daily and place in a low-traffic area.

Scratching Posts: Provide sisal or cardboard scratchers to protect furniture.

Hydration: Cats love running water; consider a stainless steel water fountain.

Shelter: Require sturdy three-sided run-in sheds or dry barn stalls.

Diet: Unlimited clean forage/hay alongside fresh non-freezing water supplies.

Hoof Care: Trim hooves every 6 to 8 weeks with a certified farrier.

Hay Supply: Timothy hay should comprise 80% of their daily diet.

Chew Toys: Provide applewood sticks or cardboard tunnels to keep teeth worn down naturally.
